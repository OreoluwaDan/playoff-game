        How to play

        Classic Race

        Everyone gets the same questions and races at their own speed. Type the answer and it checks itself. Get it right to move on. The fastest total time wins.

        Skipping in a race

        Stuck? Skip moves you on but adds 15 seconds to your time. If you stay on one question for 45 seconds, it skips for you.

        Practice

        Play by yourself and race your own best time. A grey dot shows your best pace.

        Time Attack

        You have 60 seconds. Every right answer is a point, wrong answers cost nothing, and you get one free skip. Beat your best score.
